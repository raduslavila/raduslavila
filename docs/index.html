About

Engineering systems that scale without waking people up at 3 am.

Started with microcontrollers — AVR, ARM, ESP8266, custom firmware and PCB prototypes. Kept the hardware as a hobby and went all-in on cloud-native. The 14-year arc from embedded to distributed systems isn't a detour; it's context that makes tradeoffs obvious when others are still debating abstractions.

Microsoft Orleans virtual actor framework in production Kubernetes clusters is the current specialization — research, refactoring event brokers, persistence providers (CosmosDB, Cassandra, Table Storage), SAGA frameworks on DurableTask, and multi-region AKS maintenance with SLA-backed HPA and cluster autoscaler tuning.

Casually exploring what's next: Staff / Principal Engineer or SRE roles at international companies, fully remote. NDA-constrained on portfolio specifics, but happy to talk architecture over a call.
